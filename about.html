About Me

I'm Jacky, an Asian American Computer Science student at UC San Diego. I grew up in the Bay Area and will graduate in June 2027.

I like building tools that solve real problems. My favorite so far is LectureFetch, a Chrome extension that saves lecture recordings so students can study offline. It's on the Chrome Web Store with nearly 100 users.
